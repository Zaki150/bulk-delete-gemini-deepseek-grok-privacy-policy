Privacy Policy

Bulk Delete Gemini, DeepSeek & Grok Chats (Chrome extension)
Effective date: 24 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bulk Delete Gemini, DeepSeek & Grok Chats (“the Extension”) handles your information. The Extension works entirely inside your browser. We do not collect, store, sell or share your personal data or chat content.

1. Information the Extension Accesses

  • Website content: On gemini.google.com, chat.deepseek.com and grok.com, the Extension reads your chat titles to display and manage them. It reads chat messages only when you use Deep Search, Smart Titles, Export or Share.
  • Authentication information: The Extension uses your existing signed-in session on these sites to carry out the actions you request. It never reads, collects or stores your passwords.
  • License key: If you purchase the paid version, the license key you enter is used to verify your subscription.

2. How the Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide the Extension’s features: deleting, archiving, renaming, tagging, searching, protecting and exporting your chats. All processing happens locally in your browser, and no chat data is sent to the developer.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Settings, tags, protected and archived chats, cached chat lists, the activity log, the optional weekly auto-clean setting, the free-trial counter and license status are stored only in your browser’s local extension storage. This data stays on your device and is permanently removed when you uninstall the Extension.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share your data with anyone. The only exception is license verification: when you activate the paid version, and periodically afterwards, your license key and the product ID are sent to Gumroad, our payment provider, to confirm your subscription. No other information is sent.

If you choose to share a chat, the Extension opens the app you select (such as WhatsApp, Telegram, X or email) with the text filled in. Nothing is sent until you confirm it in that app. Files you export are saved directly to your own device.

5. What We Never Do

  • Sell, rent or transfer user data to third parties.
  • Use data for advertising, profiling or to determine creditworthiness.
  • Use or transfer data for any purpose unrelated to the Extension’s single purpose.
  • Include analytics, tracking or remotely hosted code.

6. Children’s Privacy

The Extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
